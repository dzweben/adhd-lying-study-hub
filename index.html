The consent PDF has clickable boxes on the signature page for name, signature, and date.

Study Workflow

  1. Caregiver completes the Eligibility Screener.
  2. Lily emails eligible caregivers the Consent Form PDF; they sign and return it by email.
  3. Lily sends the Caregiver Questionnaire link and schedules the interview.
  4. Interview by Zoom or phone (20–40 min). Completers are entered in the raffle for one of two $20 gift cards.
